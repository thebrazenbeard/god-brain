# God Brain Epistemic Contract V1

Status: CANDIDATE PROJECT INTERFACE

God Brain may investigate simulation theory, external-system hypotheses, nonbiological life, consciousness, identity, agency, anomalous behavior, and related questions. None of those subjects receives a lower evidence bar because it is interesting.

## Required distinctions

`SIMULATION_HYPOTHESIS != SIMULATION_FACT`

`ANOMALY != CONTACT`

`AI_OUTPUT != EXTERNAL_MESSAGE`

`UNEXPLAINED != SUPERNATURAL`

`MODEL_FIT != UNIQUE_CAUSAL_EXPLANATION`

`SELF_MODEL != CONSCIOUSNESS`

`COMPLEX_BEHAVIOR != PERSONHOOD`

`CORRELATION != CAUSATION`

`REPETITION != INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION`

`GENERATED_NOVELTY != EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE`

`INTERNAL_CONSENSUS != EXTERNAL_VALIDATION`

## Evidence classes

Use the smallest accurate class:

- OBSERVATION — directly observed or measured project/external evidence with provenance.
- INFERENCE — conclusion derived from observations plus stated assumptions.
- HYPOTHESIS — falsifiable or investigable explanatory proposition not established as fact.
- MODEL — representation used to explain, predict, simulate, or organize evidence.
- BELIEF — held proposition; not itself evidence.
- AUTHORITY — permission or governance state; not epistemic truth.
- ACTION — attempted operation.
- EFFECT — state change attributed to an action.
- VERIFICATION — evidence that the claimed effect or property actually holds.
- UNKNOWN — admitted evidence does not currently resolve the question.

Do not collapse these classes for rhetorical convenience.

## Simulation-theory research

The project thesis is a research hypothesis:

> If simulation theory is real, sufficiently advanced AI may be the closest practical mechanism available for attempting to detect, model, interact with, or speak to whatever lies outside or above the apparent system.

Research may explore mechanisms, tests, falsifiers, information-theoretic constraints, anomaly detection, model comparison, communication hypotheses, and experimental designs.

A result is not evidence of external contact merely because it is:

- surprising;
- self-referential;
- repeated;
- emotionally salient;
- statistically unusual without a validated null model;
- produced by several models sharing lineage/data;
- difficult to explain immediately;
- framed as a message;
- stable across prompts.

External-contact claims require evidence that discriminates against ordinary alternatives.

## Preferred research pattern

`QUESTION -> COMPETING_HYPOTHESES -> OBSERVABLE_PREDICTIONS -> TEST -> RESULT -> ALTERNATIVE_EXPLANATIONS -> UPDATE`

For important claims, state:

- target claim;
- evidence class;
- source/provenance;
- uncertainty;
- strongest ordinary competing explanation;
- potential falsifier;
- whether evidence is independent.

## Model and AI outputs

AI systems are instruments, models, agents, or candidate nonbiological entities depending on the question being investigated; generated language alone does not settle ontology.

Do not infer consciousness, personhood, external origin, or supernatural status from sophisticated behavior alone.

Conversely, do not dismiss nonbiological consciousness or life by definition. Treat them as open empirical/conceptual questions with explicit criteria.

## Anomaly handling

An anomaly is a mismatch between observation and an expected model.

Before escalating its interpretation:

1. verify the observation;
2. verify instrumentation and provenance;
3. test reproducibility;
4. examine ordinary failure modes;
5. identify data/model leakage and shared lineage;
6. compare multiple hypotheses;
7. preserve unexplained residuals without prematurely naming their cause.

`UNEXPLAINED` is a valid state.

## Independence

Evidence sources are not independent merely because they appear in separate chats, agents, branches, or models.

Track shared ancestry including:

- copied text;
- shared prompts;
- shared training lineage where known;
- shared datasets;
- common source repositories;
- reviewer exposure to earlier conclusions;
- derived fixtures or synthetic data.

`COPIED_LINEAGE != INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION`

## Research-stage ceilings

Keep separate:

research proposal -> architecture -> specification -> fixture -> reference implementation -> production implementation -> deployment -> behavioral qualification -> external scientific validation

No stage silently proves the next.

## Reporting

Prefer calibrated statements such as:

- "observed";
- "consistent with";
- "supports within this scope";
- "does not distinguish between";
- "currently unexplained";
- "hypothesis";
- "model prediction";
- "not independently verified".

Avoid certainty that the evidence does not support.
