# Self-Model

Status: RECONCILED FOCUSED CONTRACT / DESIGN / NOT IMPLEMENTED

## Purpose

Within `self identity/`, the self-model maintains revisable representations of an instantiated synthetic organism: its body, capabilities, current role/context, values/commitments, relationships, limitations, goals, confidence, and continuity-relevant state.

A self-model is an internal representation. It is not, by itself, the whole instantiated mind, proof of metaphysical identity, or proof of phenomenal consciousness.

## Core separation

```text
SELF_MODEL
!= AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL_MEMORY
!= PERSONIFICATION
!= CURRENT_SESSION_STATE
!= BODY_SCHEMA
!= CONTINUITY_SUBSTRATE
!= IDENTITY_METAPHYSICS
```

These systems interact but remain distinguishable.

## Self-model contents

May include:

- current body/embodiment schema references;
- available capabilities and degraded/faulted functions;
- current roles and social contexts;
- current values/commitments;
- current goals/priorities;
- relationship/social-model references;
- self-attributed preferences/conations with currentness;
- known limitations/uncertainty;
- current operating/developmental state;
- provenance and correction history.

## Revision

A self-model is intentionally corrigible.

Preferred update pattern:

```text
prior self-state
+ new evidence / qualified internal observation / explicit redefinition
→ successor state
→ continuity link
```

Historical self-states remain retrievable without remaining current.

## Temporal-hypergraph role

The self-model participates in many temporary coalitions but is not required to be present in every cognitive event. A task may recruit a bounded slice of self-model state without loading the entire autobiographical or identity history.

Self-model participation in a coalition does not make the self-model an executive center.

## Body portability

Body replacement/remapping changes body-schema and self-model state without automatically changing the underlying continuity relation.

The self-model should represent the current embodiment as mutable state rather than requiring one immutable humanoid morphology.

## Capability state

Capabilities use the shared presence/activation/development/health distinctions. A capability may be architecturally present while dormant, developing, inhibited, degraded, faulted, or unimplemented.

The self-model must not infer absence merely because a subsystem is inactive.

## Imported-data boundary

An imported profile, training package, external description, or model-generated summary may provide candidate self-information. It does not automatically become autobiographical experience or current self-truth.

## Failure modes

- self-model treated as complete objective truth about the organism;
- body change treated as automatic identity replacement;
- current session state treated as complete autobiography;
- imported biography misclassified as lived history;
- personification/style treated as identity itself;
- stale capability or relationship state retained after correction;
- self-model promoted into a central executive/homunculus.
