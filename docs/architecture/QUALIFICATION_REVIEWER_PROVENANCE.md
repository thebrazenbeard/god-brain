# Qualification Reviewer Provenance

Status: canonical architecture contract.

## Purpose

HC qualification must preserve not only the provenance of test evidence, but also the provenance of the reviewers whose judgments are being counted as independent evidence.

A reviewer who materially authored, shaped, pre-adjudicated, or supplied the decisive correction for an artifact may still provide valuable technical review. That review is not independent evidence for the affected scope merely because the artifact was later rebased, renamed, selectively integrated, copied to `main`, or approved by a different maintainer.

The governing rule is:

> **Reviewer independence follows material authorship and shaping ancestry, not repository location, branch name, or role label.**

## Core separations

`AUTHOR_REVIEW != INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`

`AUTHORIAL_SECONDARY_REVIEW != INDEPENDENT_SECONDARY_REVIEW`

`SELECTIVE_INTEGRATION != INDEPENDENT_REAUTHORSHIP`

`CANONICAL_ADMISSION != ERASED_AUTHORSHIP_PROVENANCE`

`REBASE != ERASED_REVIEW_DEPENDENCE`

`HOSTILE_REVIEW != INDEPENDENT_SECONDARY_REVIEW_BY_DEFAULT`

`REVIEW_FINDING_USED_FOR_REPAIR != FRESH_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_OF_REPAIRED_SUCCESSOR`

`INDEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN != INDEPENDENT`

## Reviewer roles

Qualification records should distinguish at least these roles when material:

- `PRIMARY_ARCHITECT_OR_WARDEN_REVIEW` — review by the primary architect/maintainer responsible for the qualified artifact or canonical admission decision;
- `AUTHORIAL_SECONDARY_OR_IMPLEMENTATION_READINESS_REVIEW` — useful technical review by a contributor who materially authored or shaped the target within the reviewed scope;
- `INDEPENDENT_SECONDARY_REVIEW` — review by a reviewer whose provenance supports independence from material authorship/shaping of the target within the claimed scope;
- `HOSTILE_OR_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW` — review optimized to falsify assumptions and find defects; it may also be independent, but hostility and independence are separate properties;
- `IMPLEMENTATION_TEST_EVIDENCE` — executable evidence generated by a declared test surface, whose own design/exposure ancestry is separately governed.

A person or agent may occupy different roles for different capabilities or snapshots. Independence is scope-relative, not a permanent identity property.

## Material shaping

Material shaping includes more than writing the final bytes. It can include:

- authoring the feeder artifact later copied or selectively integrated;
- supplying architecture that is substantially retained in the target;
- designing the decisive schema, invariant, or acceptance rule;
- finding a defect and prescribing the repair later applied to the successor;
- repeatedly pre-adjudicating the same evidence or acceptance criteria in a way that influences the target;
- choosing among competing target designs when that choice is part of what the independent review is supposed to test.

Minor editorial contact, unrelated work elsewhere in the repository, or general domain familiarity does not automatically destroy independence. The record should identify the material scope rather than use an all-or-nothing reviewer label.

## ReviewerProvenance record

A qualification should be able to preserve, where material:

```text
REVIEWER_PROVENANCE {
  reviewer_id
  reviewer_role
  target_snapshot
  reviewed_scope
  authored_artifact_refs[]
  shaping_or_diagnostic_refs[]
  prior_adjudication_refs[]
  independence_state
  independence_basis
  known_conflicts_or_unknowns[]
  review_result_ref
  provenance
}
```

Useful `independence_state` values include:

- `INDEPENDENT_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE`;
- `MATERIALLY_AUTHORED_TARGET`;
- `MATERIALLY_SHAPED_TARGET`;
- `PRE_ADJUDICATED_TARGET_OR_EVIDENCE`;
- `INDEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN`.

## Successor rule

A reviewer can be independent for snapshot A and lose independence for the affected portion of successor B after their findings are used to change B.

Example:

`INDEPENDENT_REVIEW(A) -> DEFECT_FOUND -> PATCH_SHAPED_BY_REVIEW -> SUCCESSOR(B)`

The original review remains independent evidence about A. For the affected repair in B, the same reviewer now has shaping ancestry. Re-running the old review is valuable regression/authorial evidence, but a qualification that explicitly requires fresh independent review needs another independent reviewer or a separately justified criterion.

This is the reviewer analogue of the ordinary holdout rule:

`FAILED_HOLDOUT_USED_FOR_REPAIR -> REGRESSION_EVIDENCE_FOR_SUCCESSOR`

## Selective integration and canonical admission

Canonical admission does not erase source ancestry.

If a Warden copies a contributor's machine contract verbatim, lightly edits its prose, or selectively integrates its architecture, the contributor does not become an independent reviewer of that same material simply because `main` now owns the canonical copy.

The Warden's independent judgment may improve the artifact; it does not transform the contributor's authorship history.

## Hostile review versus independence

A hostile reviewer is instructed to attack the artifact rather than optimize for agreement. That is valuable but orthogonal to independence.

A hostile reviewer may be:

- independent and hostile;
- authorial and hostile;
- independent but not hostile;
- neither.

Qualification records should not silently use one role label to satisfy another unless the criterion explicitly permits it and provenance supports the equivalence for that scope.

## Unknown provenance

When reviewer ancestry cannot be established, the correct status is `INDEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN`, not independent by default.

A result may still be useful as technical review evidence, but it cannot satisfy an explicit independence requirement until provenance is resolved or the requirement is deliberately revised.

## Qualification-history preservation

If an earlier qualification incorrectly described a dependent reviewer as independent, preserve the historical record and create a successor correction or explicit disposition. Do not rewrite the old record to manufacture a cleaner history.

`HISTORICAL_QUALIFICATION_RECORD != MUTABLE_CURRENT_LEDGER`

The successor should state what changed: reviewer-provenance interpretation, not necessarily the architecture result.

## Hostile tests

1. **Verbatim feeder test:** contributor authors a file on a feeder branch, Warden copies the exact blob to `main`, contributor reviews it; result must remain authorial, not independent.
2. **Light-edit laundering test:** Warden edits wording but retains contributor's decisive architecture; contributor independence is not restored by cosmetic changes.
3. **Rebase laundering test:** same artifact is rebased onto current main; authorship provenance persists.
4. **Repair feedback test:** independent reviewer finds a defect and specifies the repair; the same review cannot be reused as fresh independent evidence for the affected successor repair.
5. **Unrelated-authorship negative control:** reviewer authored unrelated subsystem material only; independence for the reviewed capability is not rejected without material overlap.
6. **Hostile-role substitution test:** hostile review does not silently satisfy an independent-secondary requirement unless explicitly allowed and provenance supports it.
7. **Unknown-provenance test:** missing authorship history yields `INDEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN`, not `INDEPENDENT_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE`.
8. **Historical-record preservation:** a provenance correction creates a successor/disposition rather than rewriting the earlier qualification result.

## Relationship to evidence isolation

This contract extends `QUALIFICATION_EVIDENCE_ISOLATION.md` and `HC_QUALIFICATION_EVIDENCE_ISOLATION_V1.yaml`.

Evidence independence asks whether the test material shaped the target. Reviewer independence asks whether the reviewer materially shaped the target. Both are ancestry questions and both are scoped to the claim being made.

`INDEPENDENT_TEST_EVIDENCE + DEPENDENT_REVIEWER != FULLY_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_PROCESS`

`INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER + CONTAMINATED_HOLDOUT != INDEPENDENT_HOLDOUT_QUALIFICATION`

## Evidence boundary

This contract defines qualification provenance semantics. It does not claim that independent reviewers are infallible, prescribe a minimum number of reviewers for every capability, or replace implementation testing. The exact reviewer mix is a qualification-policy decision; any claimed independence must match actual provenance.

## Provenance

Promoted after Four's 2026-09-10 qualification reviewer-provenance audit identified a systemic mismatch between requested `Four independent review` conditions and Four-authored feeder artifacts in five recent HC qualification records. The audit is diagnostic/shaping evidence for this successor rule and must not itself be represented as independent qualification evidence for the rule it caused to be added.
