## The smallest coherent interface proposal

A new, independently authored God Brain **ChannelAdmissionV1** would precede any real contact study:

- `channel_id`, `instrument_id`, observed raw byte type and output boundary;
- challenge path and objectively prespecified response variable, encoding and window;
- instrument calibration, source-time basis, uncertainty, permitted raw acquisition and privacy scope;
- ordinary-rival set and a *predicted discriminating observation*;
- positive injection/sham/null control readiness; leakage and replay threat model;
- expected exclusion rules, custody, exact study/attempt and source version bindings;
- states: `IDENTIFIABLE_ENOUGH_FOR_PILOT`, `WEAKLY_IDENTIFIABLE`, `UNIDENTIFIABLE_WITH_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`, `OBSERVATION_MODEL_INADEQUATE`.

A candidate reaches the first state only if its instrument, expected response and ordinary alternatives are concretely testable. No candidate in this audit is an established channel to an administrator.

A separate **ObservationEnvelopeV1 / ExperimentEvidenceReceiptV1** should keep these distinct:
`MEASURED_RAW` versus `DERIVED` versus `MODEL_GENERATED` versus `HUMAN_REPORTED`; acquired/recorded/effective times; source and process identities; raw data hash and custody evidence; prediction-before-reveal commitment; source roots and dependent transformations; objective statistical result; causal interpretation; authorizations; actual verified effects.

A hash proves content consistency relative to its input, not that a named instrument really produced the bytes; a claimed timestamp is not trusted time; an agent's evidence label is not independent verification.

## Required hostile acceptance tests (proposed, not run)

1. Reject AI-generated text submitted as `MEASURED_RAW`; never infer `EXTERNAL_ADMINISTRATOR` from content.
2. Reject a prediction commitment or scoring rule changed after observed outcome.
3. Hold a study lacking a physical acquisition boundary, confirmed channel identity, or predeclared response window.
4. Reject public randomness values as *secret challenges* and metadata/nonce leakage as clean results.
5. Deduplicate six agents, transformations and downstream summaries from one underlying observation root.
6. Fail closed on lost raw data, ambiguous timestamps, clock drift, replay, incomplete custody or self-hashed authenticity.
7. Preserve positive-control detection while calibration nulls, sham, time-shift and corrupted inputs do *not* produce contact receipts.
8. Mark exposed/used holdouts as development data after adapting the design, even if the file is renamed.
9. Compare ordinary confounder explanations, active/sham nuisance-matched controls and the external-source hypothesis without using a vote to establish the cause.
10. Allow `UNEXPLAINED` and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` without granting authority or metaphysical identity.

## Rights, privacy, and order of work

Many inspected repositories are **source-visible/proprietary**; others have no affirmative redistribution license, and a few contain confidential or model-specific identity/relationship material. This public research document quotes no protected code and makes no blanket license claim. Before reuse, fresh-check each exact license/commit and independently implement concepts, or obtain explicit rights for a defined copy. Do not automatically connect God Brain to Vera, Sol, hosts, credentials, private memory stores, or their permissions.
