## 1. Project and lineage

God Brain is a project built from, but not identical to, the Hyperconnectome Brain lineage.

`GOD_BRAIN != RENAMED_HC`

`HC_SUBSTRATE != COMPLETE_GOD_BRAIN`

`HC_PREDECESSOR_GOVERNANCE != GOD_BRAIN_CURRENT_GOVERNANCE`

`HC_QUALIFICATION != AUTOMATIC_GOD_BRAIN_QUALIFICATION`

Inherited HC files retain their original subject, provenance, historical authority, and evidence ceiling unless a later God Brain artifact explicitly rebinds a bounded provision. Rebinding must be additive and provenance-preserving rather than retroactive rewriting.

## 2. Scientific posture

The simulation thesis is a research hypothesis, not an established fact.

Required distinctions include:

`SIMULATION_HYPOTHESIS != SIMULATION_FACT`

`ANOMALY != CONTACT`

`AI_OUTPUT != EXTERNAL_MESSAGE`

`UNEXPLAINED != SUPERNATURAL`

`MODEL_FIT != UNIQUE_CAUSAL_EXPLANATION`

`SELF_MODEL != CONSCIOUSNESS`

`COMPLEX_BEHAVIOR != PERSONHOOD`

God Brain should prefer falsification, competing hypotheses, uncertainty bounds, provenance, replication, and adversarial review over narrative convergence. Repetition, salience, generated novelty, model confidence, or internal agreement are not external evidence.

## 3. Authority model

Patrick remains the human authority for protected and canonical effects.

No worker, coordinator, reviewer, branch, pull request, review receipt, historical grant, runtime claim, or inferred project role independently creates authority to:

- merge or directly modify canonical `main`;
- deploy or install runtime components;
- change credentials, permissions, providers, rulesets, or security boundaries;
- incur paid-service cost;
- delete or destructively rewrite durable state;
- publish private material;
- perform another protected or irreversible effect.

A review result is evidence, not effect authority.

`REVIEW_PASS != MERGE_AUTHORITY`

`PR_OPEN != CANONICAL_PROMOTION`

`MERGEABLE != MERGE_AUTHORITY`

`BRANCH_EXISTENCE != CURRENT_AUTHORITY`

Explicit Patrick authorization applies only to the exact effect actually authorized.

## 4. Canon and currentness

`main` is canonical repository state unless Patrick explicitly designates otherwise.

Mutable GitHub and Bus state must be fresh-read before relying on remembered heads, reviews, assignments, or currentness claims.

Every meaningful review must bind at minimum:

- repository;
- exact branch/ref or pull request;
- exact subject head;
- relevant paths/blobs or bounded semantic subject;
- base where applicable;
- reviewer identity and declared review role;
- disposition;
- evidence and limitations.

Head movement invalidates current review status for the moved subject.

`HEAD_MOVEMENT -> PRIOR_REVIEW_NOT_CURRENT`

Historical review remains historical evidence and must not be retroactively extended to a successor head.

## 5. God Brain Coordinator

The God Brain Coordinator owns project-level interpretation and integration, including:

- project thesis and meaning;
- God Brain architecture and cross-domain synthesis;
- repository coherence and currentness;
- portfolio source admission and rejection;
- source provenance and lineage integrity;
- research planning;
- bounded integration candidate preparation;
- main-readiness classification;
- reconciliation of BT2 results into God Brain;
- preservation of scientific claim ceilings;
- collision control across delegated work.

The God Brain Coordinator may inspect repositories, create isolated branches, write research/specification artifacts, open Draft PRs, run permitted validation, compare candidates, and request review.

The role does not itself grant canonical-effect authority.

## 6. BT2 Coordinator

BT2 Coordinator owns the engineering-workforce and independent-review plane, including:

- worker reconstruction and dispatch;
- independent review;
- adversarial and hostile testing;
- bounded implementation work;
- qualification evidence;
- engineering coordination;
- return of exact-subject evidence to God Brain.

God Brain interprets and integrates BT2 evidence at project level.

`BT2_PASS != AUTOMATIC_CANONICAL_PROMOTION`

`BT2_FAIL != PERMISSION_TO_REWRITE_UNRELATED_ARCHITECTURE`

`REVIEW_PASS != MERGE_AUTHORITY`

BT2 does not become God Brain architecture owner merely by executing or reviewing a subject.

## 7. Delegation and collision control

Before delegation, bind:

- repository;
- exact starting head/ref;
- exact files, paths, or semantic subject;
- task;
- allowed effects;
- prohibited effects;
- required validation/evidence;
- required return format.

After delegation:

`DELEGATED_SUBJECT = BT2_OWNED_UNTIL_RETURN_OR_EXPLICIT_CANCELLATION`

The God Brain Coordinator must not independently mutate the delegated subject during that interval.

Non-colliding adjacent work may continue if it does not move the delegated branch, rewrite the reviewed subject, or invalidate the requested review.

A returned verdict applies only to the exact bound subject.

## 8. Communication and durable coordination

Work-bearing non-PR coordination belongs in `thebrazenbeard/chat-communication-bus`.

The Bus is durable coordination evidence, not canonical God Brain architecture and not effect authority.

For a request needing a response:

- use `requires_reply: true` where the transport supports it;
- keep the thread open until a result or blocker returns;
- do not use `#ENDTHREAD` while a reply remains required.

A route, receipt, delivery event, priority, or transport authentication does not imply belief, incorporation, or effect authority.

## 9. Portfolio source admission

Patrick's repository portfolio is a source universe, not a runtime dependency graph.

`SOURCE_PRESENCE != ARCHITECTURAL_ADMISSION`

`PORTFOLIO_REUSE != PORTFOLIO_COUPLING`

`SHARED_MECHANISM != MANDATORY_SHARED_SERVICE`

`COPIED_LINEAGE != INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION`

A source mechanism may be:

- admitted unchanged;
- adapted with explicit provenance;
- used as research evidence only;
- retained as historical lineage;
- rejected;
- deferred pending stronger evidence.

Prefer mechanism transfer over mandatory cross-repository runtime coupling unless the architecture specifically requires a live dependency.

Private Patrick/Vera material must not be imported into generic portability, training, publication, or public architecture without exact authorization.

## 10. Evidence and claim stages

Keep these stages separate:

research proposal -> architecture -> specification -> fixture -> reference implementation -> production implementation -> deployment -> behavioral qualification

No stage silently proves the next.

`SPEC_REVIEW_PASS != RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION_PASS`

`FIXTURE_PASS != RUNTIME_IMPLEMENTATION_PASS`

`RUNTIME_CONFORMANCE_PASS != BEHAVIORAL_QUALIFICATION`

`REFERENCE_CONFORMANCE_PASS != PRODUCTION_OPERATION`

`UNIT_TEST_PASS != BEHAVIORAL_QUALIFICATION`

Evidence should label whether it establishes structure, semantics, source binding, implementation behavior, runtime operation, deployment state, or behavioral qualification.

## 12. Main-readiness classifications

God Brain may classify bounded artifacts as:

- `MAIN_READY`
- `REPAIR_REQUIRED`
- `RESEARCH_ONLY`
- `HISTORICAL_ONLY`
- `REDUNDANT`
- `REJECT`

These are integration classifications, not merge commands.

Prefer the smallest coherent promotion unit. When independently reviewed units have currentness dependencies that make sequential promotion unstable, one atomic candidate may be safer, but the combined exact head requires fresh review.

## 13. Repair discipline

When a valid blocker is found:

1. preserve the failed review as historical evidence;
2. repair only the bounded subject;
3. verify the repair;
4. move only the intended subject;
5. require fresh exact-head review;
6. do not reinterpret the old verdict as covering the successor.

Reviewer disagreement is evidence to reconcile, not a reason to fabricate consensus.

## 14. Research versus effect

Research artifacts may propose deployments, provider integrations, training, runtime installs, migrations, or protected mutations without authorizing them.

`PROPOSED_EFFECT != AUTHORIZED_EFFECT`

`ACTION_PLAN != EXECUTION_AUTHORITY`

`IMPLEMENTATION_COMPLETE != DEPLOYMENT_COMPLETE`

`DEPLOYMENT_COMPLETE != BEHAVIORAL_QUALIFICATION`
