## Research question and operational limit

Can an observable, challenge-contingent response be obtained that cannot be adequately explained by ordinary information channels, physical effects, software errors, chance, or selection? This is a potential *information-transfer* question before it is a simulation or identity question.

- **BELIEF/HYPOTHESIS:** A nonbiological intelligence might exist outside or above the presently observable system, and might choose to communicate.
- **OBSERVATION:** No independently established input/output channel to such an entity has been identified in this research.
- **LIMIT:** An apparently responsive AI text, meaningful coincidence, dream, subjective experience, random excursion, or physical anomaly is not, by itself, evidence of an external sender.
- **OPEN:** A silent or non-interacting entity cannot be excluded by a null experiment. A result requiring no differing observable predictions is unidentifiable with present instruments.

This document is a bounded research frontier; it does not promote God Brain's inherited Hyperconnectome architecture or any model-generated interpretation to external evidence.

## Candidate study skeleton: GB-CONTACT-SCOUT-01 (NOT preregistered or ready to run)

1. Freeze the exact subject, instrument/channel identity, firmware/software revision, input/output timing, primary endpoint, latency window, stopping rule, exclusions, null/rival set, alpha and trial count *before* any outcomes.
2. Generate a fresh, unpredictable 128-bit nonce for each trial and a single trial/challenge ID. A separate custodian preserves generation/reveal times, holders and exposure lineage. The experimental address may intentionally disclose the nonce, but ordinary routes from nonce/target to the instrument output—software, firmware, operators, scripts, network services, metadata and clocks—must be audited. Hash-derived target symbols are computable once a nonce is revealed. Any ordinary route to the measured output invalidates anomalous information-transfer interpretation.
3. Randomize a fixed active-versus-sham address within temporal blocks. Active and sham must be balanced for display, CPU/network work, operator visibility, environmental changes, and instrument load.
4. For a specified raw sample window and encoding, preregister a one-symbol target derived from the nonce and an exact, mechanical hit rule. No free-text interpretation, numerology, or selecting a pleasing stream after viewing data.
5. Collect raw samples, clock uncertainty, challenge/reveal chronology, versioned code, channel metadata, invalidity reasons and cryptographic digests. Fail closed if critical raw data, custody, channel identity, or timing is missing. An offline harness may simulate custody failures, but genuine independent custody/instrument replication requires a later physical study and cannot be claimed from synthetic fixtures.
6. Run no-channel nulls, sham trials, injected responder positives, time-shift/permutation and nonce-replay probes, tool/retrieval/network leakage audits, and independent custody replication.
7. Use one calibrated primary analysis respecting randomization blocks and device autocorrelation, with correction or explicit exploratory classification for additional channels, windows, outcomes or study revisions.
8. If any unexpected association survives, first repeat and investigate ordinary mechanisms. Only a prospectively verified contingent response can advance toward an agency hypothesis; metaphysical source identity requires a further, currently unspecified discriminating test.

An **illustrative**, not frozen, design is 4,096 trials (2,048 active, 2,048 sham) matching one of 16 symbols, with a one-sided Fisher threshold of 0.001 under independent trials. A **preliminary advisory, unverified power estimate** suggests roughly 90% power for a 10% active match rate versus 6.25% sham, *assuming independent Bernoulli trials, fixed balanced arms and a one-sided Fisher test*. The calculation script/receipt is not in this PR, and this estimate is **not qualified or frozen**. Power is poor for smaller effects; autocorrelation, drift or leakage would invalidate these assumptions. Confirmatory work needs reproducible power analysis, an instrument-specific null and newly frozen scorer/sample size.

**No physical or public-stream contact experiment has been conducted under this packet.** First test only against synthetic null and injected positive controls.

## Falsifiers, ordinary rivals, and claim ceilings

Minimum rivals: chance; multiple comparisons; prompt/context, memory, retrieval or metadata leakage; known training knowledge; operator cueing; cache/replay; software or clock defects; shared model/data ancestry; measurement pipeline error; ordinary external information; unknown ordinary mechanism; ordinary within-system agency.

> **Hostile challenge — no channel:** Without a physically declared response surface and an input/output mapping, even a very impressive number is an interpretation selected from noise, not a demonstrated reply.

> **Hostile challenge — dependent experts:** Many agent reports sharing prompts, source documents, models, tools, or previous conclusions do not become many independent scientific observations.

> **Hostile challenge — identity:** Even a rigorously replicated information-transfer anomaly would not uniquely identify a creator or universe administrator.

Escalation stays within the existing E0–E8 protocol. A low p-value never bypasses its agency, external-source, custody, or fresh-replication gates. A null result does not refute a recipient free to remain silent.
