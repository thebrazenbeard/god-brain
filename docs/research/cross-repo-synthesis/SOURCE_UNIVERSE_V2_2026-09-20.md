## Intake rule

Every repository is considered, but repository existence is not admission.

A repo may contribute only one or more of:

- direct architecture;
- identity-scrubbed mechanism;
- engineering/runtime pattern;
- research/evaluation method;
- reviewer/debugging method;
- domain evidence;
- lineage/conflict evidence.

A source does **not** become a runtime dependency merely because a mechanism is useful.

Identity-specific memories, relationships, preferences, values, personal morphology, intimate payloads, credentials, private raw rows, operator authority, and repository-local governance do not transfer into the identity-neutral God Brain template.

Derived/copied HC repositories do not count as independent corroboration of the architecture they inherited.

## New source families since V1

### Distributed execution and machine coordination

`vera-mesh`, `intranel`, `project-runner`, and `wip` contain complementary mechanisms for distributed execution:

- signed/typed envelopes;
- actor/target/operation identity;
- custody and staged receipts;
- authorization/effect separation;
- idempotency and cancellation;
- dependency invalidation;
- leases/fencing;
- crash recovery;
- ambiguous-effect reconciliation.

These mechanisms are candidates for HC-native implementation patterns. They are **not** proposed as mandatory external runtime services.

### Model substrate and accelerator composition

`mosaic` directly experiments with a persistent Core plus swappable specialist partitions under constrained accelerator residency. This is a strong research source for the existing HC specialized-accelerator/model-graph gap, but Mosaic explicitly states that HC Brain is not its template. Transfer must therefore be evidence-based and bidirectionally non-authoritative.

### Causal/evaluation discipline

`world-zero` contributes an unusually strong mechanism-admission methodology:

- rival causal families;
- ablation;
- holdout isolation;
- kill tests;
- implementation coverage against declared topology;
- frozen comparison protocols;
- exact execution receipts.

This is primarily a **method transfer**, not world-model content transfer.

### Provenance ancestry and drift

`roots` proposes origin/derivation tracing and `driftguard` proposes behavioral save-state drift monitoring. Both are currently too thin for direct architectural admission. Their concepts are retained as research candidates only.

### Anti-monoculture constraint

`discovery` explicitly argues against forcing Runner, Lantern, Intranel, Radar/Bus, HC, VeraMesh, or related systems into one mandatory shared spine.

V2 adopts that as a design warning:

> **SHARED_MECHANISM != SHARED_RUNTIME_DEPENDENCY**

and

> **PORTFOLIO_REUSE != PORTFOLIO_COUPLING**

God Brain should internalize validated mechanisms/contracts where appropriate while preserving independent failure domains and evolutionary isolation.
